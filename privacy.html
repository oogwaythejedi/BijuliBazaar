This privacy policy explains how Bijuli Bazaar (bijulibazaar.com) collects, uses, and protects your personal information. This is placeholder legal copy — have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before launch.

We collect information you provide directly (such as your name, email address, and messages submitted through our forms) and basic technical data such as your browser type and pages visited.
