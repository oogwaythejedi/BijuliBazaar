Our story

From curiosity to the country's EV bazaar

  1. The question

    Why was Nepal — a country with world-beating hydropower — still importing petrol like there was no tomorrow?

  2. The discovery

    Nepal now leads the world in EV adoption after Norway. The vehicles had arrived; the marketplace hadn't.

  3. The bazaar

    So we built one — transparent prices, official distributors, honest comparisons, and real test rides.

  4. The future

    As charging stations double and the fleet grows, our job is to make every switch simple, fair, and informed.

What we stand for
