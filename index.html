What buyers ask us most

How much does it cost to charge at home?

A full charge for a typical scooter costs roughly NPR 60–120 [CLIENT-COPY: confirm with client] on the household tariff — less than a tank of petrol by far.

Is the real-world range lower than advertised?

Yes — like all vehicles, range varies with speed, load, and hills. We publish realistic mixed-riding figures, not just lab numbers.

Are there government subsidies for EVs?

EVs enjoy preferential import duties and taxes. Some municipalities offer additional incentives — see our Financing page for current details.
