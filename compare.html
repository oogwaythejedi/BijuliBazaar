How to read the specs

Four things that actually matter

  • Real-world range

    Match the range to your daily ride — Kathmandu–Lalitpur commutes are short; Pokhara hills are not.

  • Where you'll charge

    Home charging needs a dedicated socket. Check your parking situation before you pick a model.

  • Service network

    A great bike is worthless without a nearby service center. We show the official network for each brand.

  • Total cost, not sticker price

    Add insurance, charger, and servicing — we help you run the full number.
