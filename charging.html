Charging FAQs

The questions we hear daily

How much does a home charge cost?

A typical scooter charge costs NPR 60–120 [CLIENT-COPY: confirm with client] on household tariffs — far less than petrol.

Can I charge in an apartment?

Yes, with landlord agreement and a dedicated meter. We provide the paperwork and safe installation.

How do I find public chargers on a trip?

Charger maps are built into most EV apps. For route planning, ask us — we keep updated lists for popular corridors.

Does cold weather reduce range?

Yes — winter and mountain riding reduce range 10–20% [CLIENT-COPY: confirm with client]. We factor this into route advice.

Is fast charging safe for the battery?

Occasional fast charging is fine; daily fast charging ages batteries faster. We recommend home slow charging as the default.
